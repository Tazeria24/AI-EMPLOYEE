# Task 13 — Beta Launch

## Objective
Prepare and onboard the first beta businesses.

## Dependencies
Task 12.

## Requirements
- internal QA
- onboarding polish
- seed/demo business
- install guide
- support process
- feedback capture
- activation checklist
- 5–10 beta businesses
- release candidate checklist

## Non-goals
Do not add major features during launch unless blocking adoption.

## Acceptance criteria
A new business can onboard without founder intervention beyond expected setup; AI conversation works end-to-end; human takeover works; core monitoring is active; known issues are documented.
