# Task 07 — Leads

## Objective
Turn buying intent into a manageable sales pipeline.

## Dependencies
Task 06.

## Requirements
- leads
- lead events
- statuses NEW/CONTACTED/INTERESTED/NEGOTIATING/WON/LOST
- lead detail
- source/intent/score
- conversation-to-lead creation
- activity timeline

## Acceptance criteria
AI can create a lead through a tool; business can manage pipeline; events are recorded; permissions and RLS are correct.
