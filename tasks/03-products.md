# Task 03 — Products

## Objective
Build product catalog management.

## Dependencies
Task 02.

## Requirements
- product categories
- product CRUD
- price/currency
- SKU
- stock quantity
- active/inactive status
- search/filter
- organization scoping

## Acceptance criteria
Business can create, edit, archive and search products; validation works; RLS protects products; UI has loading/empty/error states.
