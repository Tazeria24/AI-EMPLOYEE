# Product Specification

## Product
AI Sales & Customer-Service Employee for SMEs.

## Initial ICP
Nigerian online retailers, especially businesses selling through:
- websites
- Instagram
- WhatsApp

Potential verticals:
- fashion
- beauty
- hair
- electronics
- furniture
- food
- other catalog-driven merchants

## Problem
Owners and staff repeatedly answer:
- price questions
- availability questions
- size/color questions
- delivery questions
- location/hours questions
- ordering questions
- policy questions

Slow replies cause lost leads. Manual follow-up is inconsistent.

## Promise
Give a business an AI employee that responds to customers, recommends products, captures leads and follows up automatically.

## Core capabilities
1. Business knowledge management
2. Product/catalog search
3. AI customer conversations
4. Product recommendations
5. Lead capture and qualification
6. Human takeover
7. Automated follow-ups
8. Website chat widget
9. WhatsApp channel
10. Basic analytics
11. Subscription/usage management

## Core journey
Business signs up -> creates business profile -> adds products/FAQs/policies -> activates AI -> installs website widget -> customer chats -> AI answers using verified data -> detects buying intent -> captures lead -> follows up -> escalates when needed -> business sees conversation/lead/conversion.

## Product principles
- Useful before impressive.
- Accuracy before autonomy.
- Human handoff before pretending certainty.
- Business-owned knowledge is the source of truth.
- One niche first, broader SME platform later.

## MVP
Website widget + dashboard + product catalog + knowledge base + RAG + AI tools + conversations + leads + follow-ups + human takeover + basic analytics + billing.

WhatsApp is included only after the core conversation engine is stable.

## Success metrics
Activation:
- business completes profile
- adds products
- adds knowledge
- installs widget

AI:
- conversations handled
- grounded answer rate
- escalation rate
- tool success rate

Sales:
- leads captured
- qualified leads
- follow-ups sent
- conversions

Business:
- weekly active businesses
- retention
- MRR
- support time saved

## Initial pricing hypothesis
Starter: ₦15,000/month
Growth: ₦35,000/month
Pro: ₦75,000/month

Treat these as hypotheses. Validate with real customers.

## Long-term expansion
AI Customer Service -> AI Sales -> AI Marketing -> AI Operations -> AI Finance Assistant -> broader AI OS.

Do not implement these future layers during MVP unless they are directly required for the current customer workflow.
